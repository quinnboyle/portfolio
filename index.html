About

A designer who
thinks in systems
and speaks code.

I'm a UX designer with a background in IT support at ICON PLC, where I saw firsthand how good (and bad) software design impacts people's daily work. That experience sparked my transition into UX—I wanted to be on the front lines of creating better experiences, not just fixing broken ones.

I approach design through a research-first lens, combining user insights with technical feasibility. My IT background means I understand both what users need and what's actually buildable—which helps me design solutions that ship.

Currently seeking entry-level UX roles in healthcare, education, or sustainability — domains where thoughtful design makes a real difference.
